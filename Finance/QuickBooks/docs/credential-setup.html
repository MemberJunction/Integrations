Connection setup

Connect your QuickBooks Online company

Create an app in the Intuit Developer portal, then use Intuit's OAuth Playground to authorize your company and mint tokens. About fifteen minutes — you'll send us five values at the end.

OAuth 2.0 5 values to send ~15 minutes

Before you start

You'll need a free Intuit Developer account (developer.intuit.com) and admin access to the QuickBooks Online company you want to connect. When you're done you'll send us five values: a Client ID and Client Secret (from your app), a Realm ID and a Refresh token (from the OAuth Playground), and whether the company is a Sandbox or Production company. QuickBooks uses the same accounting scope, com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting, throughout.

Read this first · Development vs Production

The Intuit portal has two separate environments, and every keys/settings page has a Development ⁄ Production toggle at the top. They have different Client IDs and Secrets. Pick one and use the same toggle on every screen:

  • Development → connects to a free sandbox QuickBooks company (fake data — perfect for a first test). Send us Environment: Sandbox.
  • Production → connects to your real QuickBooks company (live data). Send us Environment: Production. Production keys require completing Intuit's short app-details checklist first.

Heads-up: the screenshots below happen to show different toggles (some Development, some Production) because they were captured across both — ignore which tab is lit in the picture and always use the one you chose. Your Client ID/Secret, Realm ID, and tokens must all come from the same environment; mixing them is the #1 reason a connection fails.

No sandbox company yet? Intuit gives every developer one free — create or view it at developer.intuit.com/app/developer/sandbox (also under Dashboard → Sandbox).

Part 1 · Create your app
  1. 1

    Sign in to Intuit Developer

    Go to developer.intuit.com and sign in with your Intuit account (the same login you use for QuickBooks). If you don't have a developer account yet, it's free to create.

  2. 2

    Open your workspace

    After signing in you'll land in your developer workspace. This is where your apps live — you'll create one here.

  3. 3

    Create an app

    Click Create an app to start a new app. This app is just the container that holds your API keys — you won't publish anything.

  4. 4

    Choose QuickBooks Online

    When asked what you're building, choose the QuickBooks Online and Payments platform and click Get started.

  5. 5

    Name your app

    Give the app any name you like (1) — e.g. MemberJunction Sync — then click Create app (2).

  6. 6

    Select the accounting scope

    Under the app's Permissions, tick com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting (1) and click Save (2). This is the only scope the connector needs.

  7. 7

    Copy your Client ID and Client Secret

    Open Keys and credentials. First set the environment toggle (red arrow) to the one you chose above — Development for a sandbox company, Production for a real one (1). Then click Show credentials (2) and copy the Client ID and Client Secret from the boxed block (3). Keep these two safe; you'll send them to us. Remember which toggle you used — every remaining step must use the same one.

  8. 8

    Add the Playground redirect URL

    The OAuth Playground you'll use next needs to be a registered redirect. In your app's Settings → Redirect URIs (on the same environment tab you picked in step 7), paste this exact value into the field (1) and click Add URI (2):

    https://developer.intuit.com/v2/OAuth2Playground/RedirectUrl

Part 2 · Authorize & get tokens
  1. 9

    Open the OAuth 2.0 Playground

    Go to the OAuth 2.0 Playground and select the app you just created from the dropdown, so it uses your Client ID and Secret.

  2. 10

    Get an authorization code

    Make sure the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope is ticked (1), then click Get authorization code (2).

  3. 11

    Connect your company

    Intuit asks which company to authorize. Pick the QuickBooks company you want to sync and click Connect. (In Development mode you'll see your sandbox company here.)

  4. 12

    Note the Realm ID, then exchange for tokens

    You're returned to the Playground. Copy the Realm ID shown here (1) — that's your company's ID, and we'll need it. Then click Get tokens (2) to exchange the authorization code for your tokens.

  5. 13

    Copy the Refresh token

    The Playground now shows a Refresh token (1) and an Access token (2). Copy the Refresh token — that's the one we need (it's valid ~100 days and our connector rotates it automatically). The Access token is optional and expires in 60 minutes; include it only if it's handy.

You're done
