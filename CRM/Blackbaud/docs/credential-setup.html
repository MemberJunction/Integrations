Before you start

Sign in to the Blackbaud developer portal with the same account you use for Raiser's Edge NXT (or your Blackbaud environment). The developer account is free. When you're done you'll have three values to send us — an Application ID, an Application Secret, and a Subscription Key — plus one quick authorization link to click that finishes the connection.

Blackbaud keeps two different things in two different places, and they're easy to mix up. Your Subscription Key comes from My subscriptions; your Application ID and Application Secret come from My applications. We'll walk you to each one — just keep them clearly labeled as you copy them.

  1. 1

    Sign in to the Blackbaud developer portal

    Go to developer.blackbaud.com and sign in with your Blackbaud account — the same login you use for Raiser's Edge NXT. If you've never opened a developer account, choose Sign up first; it's free and takes a minute.

  2. 2

    Open My Subscriptions and subscribe to the SKY API

    Open your account menu and choose My subscriptions. Find SKY API and click Subscribe. Scroll through and accept the terms, then submit — you'll get a quick confirmation email.

  3. 3

    Copy your Subscription Key

    Back on My subscriptions, open your SKY API subscription and scroll to the bottom. Copy the Primary access key — that's your Subscription Key, and it's the first value to send us. (There's also a Secondary key; either one works, but send the primary.)

  4. 4

    Open My Applications and register a new app

    Open your account menu and choose My applications, then click Register app. Give it a Name you'll recognize later — for example “MemberJunction.” A description and logo are optional.

  5. 5

    Add the redirect URI we give you

    In the app's Settings, find Redirect URIs and add the exact web address MemberJunction sends you. It has to match character-for-character, so paste it as-is — then Save.

    Paste the redirect URI exactly as we provide it:

    • https://<the redirect URL MemberJunction gives you>
  6. 6

    Copy the Application ID and Application Secret

    On the application page, copy the Application ID — that's your client ID. Then find the Primary application secret and copy it (generate one if the page asks) — that's your client secret. These are the second and third values to send us.

  7. 7

    Click the one-time authorization link

    The final piece is a one-time sign-in that lets us connect on your behalf. MemberJunction will email you a one-click authorization link — open it, sign in with your Blackbaud account, and approve the read-only access on the consent screen. That securely finishes the connection and creates the refresh token for us automatically, so there's nothing to copy.

You're done
